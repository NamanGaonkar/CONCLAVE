# Conclave v0.5.1

**A local virtual office of Claude Code, Antigravity, Codex, Gemini, Cursor, Grok & Copilot agents that run themselves.**
Autonomous multi-agent orchestration, messaging, routing, and memory graph, running completely local-first and offline on your machine.

---

## What's new in Conclave v0.5.1

- **Interactive Virtual Office Floor Restored:** The beloved pixel office is back! Watch your agents seated at their monitors, walking to the coffee machine, taking cafeteria breaks, and running errands.
- **Amber Yellow Light Theme:** Default warm cream surfaces (`#FFFDF5`, `#FFF8E7`, `#F4E9C7`) accented with a rich, vibrant amber yellow (`#F5AF28`).
- **Crisp Lato Typography:** Lato font integration across UI elements combined with pixel display headers.
- **Settings Clean-Up:** Completely removed Pro paywalls, Founders' Wall banners, and external promo buttons.
- **Automated Update Checking:** Native update notifications powered directly by `NamanGaonkar/CONCLAVE` releases.
- **Modernized Vocabulary:** "Lead", "agents", and "Conclave" terminology throughout.
